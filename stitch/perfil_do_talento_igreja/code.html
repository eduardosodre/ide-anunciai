About Elena

Elena Sterling is a worship leader, songwriter, and speaker based in Nashville, TN. With a heart for authentic connection, her ministry focuses on creating spaces where people can encounter the presence of God through music and truth-telling.

For over a decade, Elena has partnered with local churches and national conferences to lead congregations in worship. Her style blends atmospheric acoustic arrangements with deep, theological lyrical content. When she's not traveling, she serves as the Creative Arts Director at The Anchor Fellowship.
